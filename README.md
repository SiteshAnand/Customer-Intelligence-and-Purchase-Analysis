## RFM Customer Segmentation & Market Basket Analysis

This portfolio demonstrates how **customer transaction data can be transformed into actionable business insights** using two complementary analytical approaches:

1. **RFM Customer Segmentation** — understanding **customer value and behavior**
2. **Market Basket Analysis** — understanding **product purchasing relationships**

Together, these projects address a broader business objective:

> **Identify the right customers to target and determine what products can be offered to them to increase retention, engagement, and revenue.**

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

Businesses generate large volumes of transaction data, but transaction records alone do not answer important business questions.

Management needs to understand:

* Who are our most valuable customers?
* Which customers are loyal and should be retained?
* Which valuable customers may be at risk of becoming inactive?
* Which customers have potential to become high-value customers?
* Which products are frequently purchased together?
* What products can be bundled or cross-sold?
* How can customer and product insights be converted into revenue opportunities?

This portfolio addresses these questions through **customer segmentation and product association analysis**.

---

# 🔹 Project 1 — RFM Customer Segmentation

## Business Problem

Not every customer contributes the same value to a business.

Without customer segmentation, marketing and retention activities may treat all customers equally, resulting in inefficient allocation of promotional and retention resources.

The business needs a way to identify:

* High-value customers
* Loyal customers
* Potential customers
* Customers requiring re-engagement
* Customers who contribute significant revenue

## 💡 Business Solution

The project applies **RFM Analysis** using:

* **Recency** — How recently did the customer purchase?
* **Frequency** — How often did the customer purchase?
* **Monetary** — How much value did the customer generate?

Customers were segmented based on their purchasing behavior and value.

The analysis was developed in **KNIME** and further analyzed through **Power BI**.

## 📊 Key Business Applications

The segmentation framework can support:

* Customer retention
* Loyalty programs
* Targeted marketing
* Customer reactivation
* Cross-selling and upselling
* Customer value prioritization

### Example Business Actions

| Customer Insight                 | Recommended Action          |
| -------------------------------- | --------------------------- |
| High-value & recently active     | Retain and reward           |
| Frequent & valuable customers    | Loyalty and upselling       |
| Recent but lower-value customers | Encourage repeat purchases  |
| Previously valuable but inactive | Win-back campaigns          |
| Low-value / inactive customers   | Cost-effective reactivation |

## 📈 Key Outcome

The analysis converts transaction-level customer data into a **customer prioritization framework**, helping businesses decide **who should receive attention and what type of action should be taken**.

# 🔹 Project 2 — Market Basket Analysis

## Business Problem

Businesses often know what customers purchase, but may not understand **which products are commonly purchased together**.

This limits opportunities for:

* Cross-selling
* Product bundling
* Personalized recommendations
* Promotional planning
* Increasing average transaction value

## 💡 Business Solution

Market Basket Analysis was used to identify **product associations and purchasing patterns** within transaction data.

The analysis helps answer questions such as:

* Which products are frequently purchased together?
* Which product combinations have strong associations?
* Which products could be recommended together?
* What opportunities exist for bundling and cross-selling?

The analysis uses association-based techniques to identify meaningful relationships between products.

## 📊 Key Business Applications

The results can support:

* Product recommendations
* Cross-selling
* Upselling
* Bundle creation
* Promotional campaigns
* Store/product placement decisions
* Increasing basket size

### Example Business Actions

| Insight                                | Recommended Action                  |
| -------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Products frequently purchased together | Create product bundles              |
| Strong product association             | Recommend complementary products    |
| High-value product combination         | Promote as a package                |
| Frequently co-purchased products       | Use targeted cross-selling          |
| Complementary products                 | Create personalized recommendations |

## 📈 Key Outcome

The analysis transforms individual transaction records into **product relationship insights**, helping businesses understand **what products can be promoted together to increase customer basket value**.

# 🔄 Why These Two Projects Work Together

The strength of this portfolio is that the two analyses answer **different but connected business questions**.

### RFM Analysis

**WHO should we focus on?**

Identifies customers based on their:

**Recency + Frequency + Monetary Value**

### Market Basket Analysis

**WHAT should we offer them?**

Identifies:

**Product Associations + Purchasing Patterns**

### Combined Business Strategy

These insights can be combined to create a more targeted customer strategy.

For example:

> **RFM identifies a high-value customer → Market Basket Analysis identifies complementary products → Business creates a targeted cross-sell or bundle offer.**

This moves the analysis from simply describing historical transactions toward **actionable customer and revenue strategy**.
