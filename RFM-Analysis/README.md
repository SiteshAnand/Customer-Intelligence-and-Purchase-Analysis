# RFM Customer Segmentation & Customer Value Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer behavior analysis helps businesses identify their most valuable customers, understand purchasing patterns, and develop targeted retention strategies.

This project applies **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) Analysis** to segment customers based on their purchasing behavior and identify opportunities for customer retention, engagement, and revenue growth.

The analysis was developed using **KNIME** for data preparation and RFM segmentation and **Power BI** for interactive business analysis and visualization.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project is to answer key customer analytics questions:

* Who are the most valuable customers?
* Which customers are highly engaged and loyal?
* Which valuable customers may be at risk of becoming inactive?
* Which recent customers have the potential to become high-value customers?
* How is customer revenue distributed across different segments?
* Where should the business focus its retention and marketing efforts?

## 🔎 RFM Methodology

RFM analysis evaluates customers using three behavioral dimensions:

### Recency

Measures **how recently a customer made a purchase**.

* Lower Recency → More recent purchase
* Higher Recency → Customer purchased longer ago

### Frequency

Measures **how often a customer purchased** during the analysis period.

* Higher Frequency → More frequent purchasing behavior
* Lower Frequency → Less frequent purchasing behavior

### Monetary

Measures the **total value generated by the customer**.

* Higher Monetary → Higher customer value
* Lower Monetary → Lower customer value

Customers were classified into **Low, Medium, and High** categories for each RFM dimension.

---

## 👥 Customer Segmentation

Based on the RFM combinations, customers were categorized into meaningful business segments.

### 🏆 Champions

Customers with:

* Low Recency
* High Frequency
* High Monetary value

These customers are recent, frequent, and high-value buyers.

**Business Action:** Focus on retention, loyalty programs, premium offerings, and cross-selling.

### ⭐ Loyal / Valuable Customers

Customers showing strong purchasing frequency and monetary contribution, with relatively recent activity.

**Business Action:** Strengthen engagement and encourage repeat purchases and upselling.

### 🎯 Potential Customers

Customers with relatively recent purchases but lower frequency and/or monetary contribution.

**Business Action:** Use targeted campaigns and personalized offers to increase purchase frequency and customer value.

### ⚠️ At-Risk Valuable Customers

Previously valuable customers showing weaker recency.

**Business Action:** Prioritize re-engagement campaigns, personalized communication, and retention offers.

## 💡 Key Business Questions

### 1. Which customer segments contribute the most revenue?

Helps identify the customer groups that have the greatest financial impact on the business.

### 2. Which valuable customers are at risk?

Combines customer value with recency to identify customers who previously generated strong value but have not purchased recently.

### 3. Which recent customers have potential?

Identifies customers who have recently purchased but have not yet developed high purchasing frequency or monetary value.

### 4. Where should retention efforts be prioritized?

Customers can be prioritized based on a combination of:

**Recency + Frequency + Monetary Value**

This enables more targeted customer engagement rather than treating all customers equally.

## 🧠 Business Recommendations

Based on the segmentation framework, businesses can adopt differentiated customer strategies:

| Customer Type        | Recommended Strategy                                     |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- |
| Champions            | Retain, reward, cross-sell and build loyalty             |
| Loyal / Valuable     | Increase engagement and encourage higher-value purchases |
| Potential            | Convert into repeat and high-value customers             |
| At-Risk Valuable     | Launch targeted win-back campaigns                       |
| Low-Value / Inactive | Use cost-effective reactivation campaigns                |

The key principle is to **allocate marketing and retention resources according to customer value and behavior**.
